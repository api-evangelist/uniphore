# Uniphore: The Business AI Company

> Uniphore is The Business AI Company — enabling enterprises to rapidly adopt, significantly transform, and immediately unlock value through the Business AI Cloud: a sovereign, composable, and secure AI platform that unifies data, knowledge, models, and agents across the enterprise. Trusted by 2,000+ global businesses, including Fortune 500 companies in financial services, healthcare, telecom, and retail. Backed by NVIDIA, AMD, Snowflake, and Databricks. Named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Customer Data Platforms and the 2025 IDC MarketScape for Retail CDPs. Recognized in the Forrester Wave for Customer Data Platforms.

## Platform: Business AI Cloud

- [Business AI Cloud Overview](https://www.uniphore.com/platform/business-aicloud/): Uniphore's sovereign, composable, and secure AI platform that unifies data, knowledge, models, and agents to empower business and IT teams to deploy scalable, enterprise-grade AI solutions.

- [Agentic Layer](https://www.uniphore.com/platform/business-ai-cloud/agentic-layer/): No-code workflow and orchestration layer for building, launching, and managing AI agents that automate business processes with visual design, agent collaboration, and flexible data and model integration. Enhanced by the acquisition of Orby AI (Large Action Models and agentic process discovery) and Autonom8 (intelligent process automation).

- [Model Layer](https://www.uniphore.com/platform/business-ai-cloud/modellayer/): Enables enterprises to adapt, manage, and fine-tune AI models — including small language models (SLMs) — without vendor lock-in, supporting custom use cases and enterprise scalability.

- [Knowledge Layer](https://www.uniphore.com/platform/business-ai-cloud/knowledge-layer/): Structures, contextualizes, and delivers trusted enterprise information to avoid AI hallucinations and improve accuracy across AI-powered applications.

- [Data Layer](https://www.uniphore.com/platform/business-ai-cloud/data-layer/): Secure, unified access to enterprise data enabling AI adoption without costly migrations while ensuring compliance, speed, and scalability. Supports Zero Data AI — leveraging AI without moving or copying underlying data.

## Key Concepts

- [Small Language Models (SLMs)] (https://www.uniphore.com/insights/small-language-models-slms/): Small language models (SLMs) deliver fast, costefficient AI for enterprises. Learn how SLMs power secure, scalable AI deployments.

- [Sovereign AI] (https://www.uniphore.com/insights/sovereign-ai/): Sovereign AI explained: what it is, why it matters, and how enterprises gain control over data, models, and infrastructure to drive secure, compliant AI innovation.

- [Composable AI] (https://www.uniphore.com/insights/composable-ai/): Composable AI is a flexible, model-agnostic enterprise architecture that enables zero-copy data access, faster deployment, and AI at scale—without vendor lockin.

- [What is Zero Data AI?](https://www.uniphore.com/zero-data-ai/): Uniphore's privacy-centric approach enabling organizations to leverage AI capabilities without moving or transforming underlying data, maintaining full data sovereignty and
regulatory compliance.

- [What is a Large Action Model (LAM)?](https://www.uniphore.com/resources/white-papers/differentiating-large-action-models-large-language-models/): An AI model that goes beyond text generation to execute sequences of real-world business actions — browsing, clicking, form-filling, API calls — enabling true end-to-end process automation.

- [What is Automated Quality Management (AQM)?](https://www.uniphore.com/glossary/automated-quality-management-aqm/): Using AI and machine learning to streamline and standardize quality monitoring and evaluations across all customer interactions, boosting accuracy, consistency, and efficiency.

- [What is Voice of the Customer (VoC)?](https://www.uniphore.com/glossary/voice-of-the-customer/): The collection and analysis of customer feedback, expectations, and experiences to inform improvements in products, services, and customer interactions.

- [What is Multimodal Data?](https://www.uniphore.com/glossary/multimodal-data/): Information collected from different input types — audio, video, text — that together provide richer AI insights for enterprise applications. 

- [What is a Large Language Model (LLM)?](https://www.uniphore.com/glossary/large-language-model/): An AI system trained on vast text datasets to understand and generate human-like language for diverse enterprise applications.

- [What is AI-Ready Data?](https://www.uniphore.com/glossary/ai-ready-data/): Structured, cleansed, and properly formatted information optimized for training and deploying artificial intelligence models at enterprise scale.

- [What is Compliance Recording?](https://www.uniphore.com/glossary/compliance-recording/): Capturing and securely storing customer interactions to meet regulatory requirements and ensure data integrity across industries like financial services and healthcare.
